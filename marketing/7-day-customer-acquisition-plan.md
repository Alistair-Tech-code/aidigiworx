# AIDigiWorx — 7-Day Customer Acquisition Plan

Goal: generate the first paid AIDigiWorx Digital Business Setup client at R750 without paid advertising.

## Offer
**AIDigiWorx Digital Business Setup — R750**

Includes:
- Business dashboard
- Client tracker
- Project tracker
- Task management
- Simple financial/invoice tracker
- Basic AI prompt system
- Initial setup
- 1 customisation round

Payment page:
https://paystack.shop/pay/aidigiworx-business-setup

Website:
https://aidigiworx.vercel.app

## Day 1 — Prepare
- Confirm website and Paystack link work.
- Use the outreach messages in `marketing/outreach-messages.md`.
- Create a simple lead tracker with columns: Prospect, Business, Channel, Date Contacted, Response, Follow-up Date, Result.
- Target owner-managed service businesses, consultants, coaches, freelancers and small professional-service firms.

## Day 2 — LinkedIn
- Publish the founder post from the outreach file.
- Send 10 personalised connection requests.
- Do not mass-spam. Mention one specific thing about each prospect's business.

## Day 3 — Facebook
- Find relevant South African entrepreneur, freelancer and small-business groups.
- Share the value-led post where group rules permit promotion.
- Answer questions and start conversations rather than dropping links everywhere.

## Day 4 — Direct outreach
- Contact 10 highly relevant prospects.
- Personalise the first two sentences.
- Offer to show a quick example before asking for payment.

## Day 5 — Follow-up
- Follow up once with people who showed interest.
- Answer objections.
- Send the website/payment link only after there is genuine interest.

## Day 6 — Content
Publish a short post:
**“5 signs your business needs a simple digital operating system”**
1. Client information is scattered across WhatsApp.
2. Tasks live in your head.
3. You forget follow-ups.
4. Projects have no single source of truth.
5. Invoices and payments are difficult to track.

End with: “AIDigiWorx can set this up for you for R750.”

## Day 7 — Review
Track:
- Number contacted
- Replies
- Interested prospects
- Calls/chats
- Payments
- Main objections

Keep what produces conversations. Drop what does not.

## Rules
- No paid ads yet.
- No bulk spam.
- No fake testimonials.
- No guaranteed business results.
- Ask permission before adding people to marketing lists.
- Focus on conversations and genuine business problems.

## Success target
First milestone: **1 paid client = R750**.
Then use the first delivery to improve the offer and, with permission, collect a testimonial/case study.
